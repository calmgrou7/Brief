Clear roles

Who does what

Stay B provides general information and runs student contact. All individual advice and representation are done under the partner agent's name.

6-step flow

From enquiry to follow-up

  1. 1Stay B

    Intake

    Students ask through the web form, WhatsApp or Viber.

  2. 2Stay B

    Sorting

    Everyday questions are answered from the FAQ. Visa and stay questions go to a partner agent, with the student's consent.

  3. 3Stay B

    Getting ready

    The student shares basic information, such as visa type and expiry date, in a simple form.

  4. 4Partner agent

    Agent consultation

    The agent consults with the student directly. Quotes and engagement are agreed between the student and the agent.

  5. 5Together

    Progress updates

    When the agent shares progress, the student gets an automatic update.

  6. 6Together

    Follow-up

    Reminders before the next expiry date. The FAQ is reviewed and improved together.

Principles

Partnership principles

No fee splitting

Fees for administrative work are agreed and paid only between the student and the partner agent. Stay B does not take any share of those fees.

No administrative work by the platform

Stay B does not judge individual cases, write or file documents, or collect fees for administrative work. Students pay partner agents directly.

Consent first

Student information is passed to a partner agent only when the question needs one, and only with the student's consent.

Terms agreed together, after legal review

How we work with partner agents will be agreed together after legal review. Everything on this page is a proposal for discussion.

Stay B. Busan · Gyeongnam

For students: Stay B home

Stay B provides general information based on public sources, offline settling-in support, and a student community and consultation intake. It is not a government agency or an administrative agent's office. Stay B does not judge individual cases, prepare or file documents on anyone's behalf, or charge fees for administrative work. Those services are provided only by qualified partner administrative agents, under their own name and responsibility. Job introductions are handled by registered employment agencies. Stay B does not provide matchmaking or marriage brokering.
