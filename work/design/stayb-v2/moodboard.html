Alien registration: what to bring

If you stay in Korea longer than 90 days, you register with immigration. Book early.

Key points
  • Deadline: within 90 days of arrival to be confirmed
  • Book a visit online through HiKorea to be confirmed
  • Your card arrives a few weeks later. Keep your receipt until then.
Official source · HiKorea · Busan Immigration Office

10

Do not하지 않을 것

  • Purple gradients, emoji section markers, everything centered, the same shadow on every block
  • Navy and gold, seals, shields, scales: anything that looks like a government or law office
  • Photos of people, stock or generated. Scenes use objects: passport, transport card, SIM, kit
  • WhatsApp or Viber logos and brand colors: channel names as text only
  • BRIDGENODE logo image: footer text only until the original file arrives

Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, Figtree, Noto Sans KR, Noto Sans Devanagari, Gaegu (Google Fonts, SIL OFL). Icons and illustrations: drawn inline for this project.
