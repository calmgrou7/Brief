Official student resources

Where can international students find official information?

Use these sources to check scholarship notices, research housing or find the office responsible for a question about your stay. Your university’s international office can help you find the campus-specific details.

Where can I find Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) notices?

Study in Korea publishes information about the Global Korea Scholarship. Open the notice for your degree programme and application year to check the route and deadline.

Study in Korea · GKS →

How do I start looking for student housing?

Start with the housing guide on Study in Korea. For rooms available on a particular campus, go to the university website and check its dormitory notice.

Study in Korea · Living and housing →

Where is the English health insurance guide?

The National Health Insurance Service has an English guide for foreign residents. Use the official page to check the enrolment rules that apply to your circumstances.

National Health Insurance Service →

Who can answer a question about my stay in Korea?

The Korea Immigration Service lists the Immigration Contact Center (1345). Use its official guide for hours, languages and how to call from abroad.
